Astral Sigil 1.0.0

INSTALLATION
Install this archive through your mod manager and choose Full or Lite, a runtime option for Full, and 2K or 4K textures. Enable AstralSigil.esp.
When replacing an earlier installation, replace its mod folder rather than merging files. Disable separate casting comparison or MCM update packages; their changes are included here. Restart Skyrim, load your character and open MCM after upgrading.

FULL
Requires SkyUI, SKSE64, Address Library and Open Animation Replacer. Use dependency versions matching your SkyrimSE.exe and storefront. MCM Helper is not required.
Includes aimed third-person casting poses for the player using Stormheart or Cinderfang in the right hand while standing or walking without a left-hand staff. Other casting configurations use your installed animation setup.
G switches the drawn staff and one-handed mace; this key is configurable in MCM. R draws or puts away either form through a portal. The left hand remains independent.
MCM provides 5 sliders, 8 toggles and a keyboard binding, each with a description. Settings are saved with your character.

LITE
Includes staves, casting and impact effects, discovery and the quest journal. Uses your installed casting animations. Does not require SKSE, SkyUI or OAR. Portals, transformation, aimed-pose overrides and MCM are Full features.

GAME VERSION
SE 1.5.97 option: Steam Skyrim Special Edition 1.5.97, SKSE64 2.0.20 and Address Library SE.
SE / AE option: the exact Steam/GOG versions listed in runtime-policy.json, with matching SKSE64 and Address Library.
Select the runtime option using your SkyrimSE.exe version. The installer does not detect the executable version automatically.
The native Full modules target Steam/GOG Skyrim Special Edition. LE, VR, Epic Games Store and Game Pass are not supported by the Full modules.

GAMEPLAY
Both staves charge for four seconds, can be held ready, and fire when the casting button is released after charging. Releasing early cancels the cast.
Stormheart briefly freezes the directly struck target; native paralysis immunity and frost resistance apply. Area damage is separate from this freeze.
MCM ground-effect lifetime controls new ground flames and ice. Decal lifetime follows Skyrim and any installed decal settings.
Enchanted item values before barter modifiers: Stormheart 2000 gold; Cinderfang 3000 gold.

UPGRADING FROM SPLIT-PLUGIN PACKAGES
Use only AstralSigil.esp. Remove the old AstralSigil_MCM.esp and AstralSigil_Legacy.esp. For characters using those old auxiliary plugins, use a new game or a save from before they were installed.
